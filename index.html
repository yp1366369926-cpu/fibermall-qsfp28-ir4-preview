100G QSFP28 CWDM4 Optical Transceiver Module (SMF, 2 km, Duplex LC, DDM)

This QSFP28 module multiplexes four 25.78 Gb/s optical lanes onto a single fiber pair for 100 Gigabit Ethernet. The CWDM channels operate at 1271, 1291, 1311 and 1331 nm. With FEC, the link supports distances up to 2 km over single-mode fiber.
